Contact Experience Redesign: From Email-First to Channel-First

About this project
Situation

VistaPrint's Contact Us experience hadn't been meaningfully redesigned in years. Customers were defaulting to email — the slowest and lowest-value channel — not by preference, but because the interface made it the path of least resistance. Email contacts carry significantly lower VGP than callback (C2C), and email volume was overindexed relative to stated customer preference data. Leadership aligned on a seven-figure annual revenue key result tied specifically to contact channel mix improvement.

Task

Lead end-to-end UX research and design for a full redesign of the contact channel surface. Scope: analytics review, transcript analysis of 30 support interactions, competitive benchmarking of 15 companies, structured evaluation of 4 solution directions, high-fidelity design for desktop and mobile, unmoderated usability testing with 10 participants, and a full A/B/C test specification. Target: ship as P0 in Q3 FY26.

Action
  • Reviewed analytics for baseline channel mix, contact volume by type, and drop-off rates on the existing contact page
  • Deep-read 30 customer support transcripts — identified 5 recurring pain patterns including symptom-vs-root-cause confusion and wrong channel selection
  • Benchmarked 15 companies across e-commerce, SaaS, and consumer services — identified 4 patterns: proactive triage, self-service prioritisation, multi-channel transparency, gated contact
  • Evaluated 4 solution directions against 5 criteria (deflection power, friction, assurance, technical complexity, P0 feasibility) — selected Direction 4 (Channel-First) for P0
  • Designed the full experience across 4 states: Help Center banner, channel selection, 2-step callback form, and success states
  • Ran unmoderated usability testing with 10 participants via UserTesting — validated core patterns, refined email hierarchy and callback confirmation
  • Specified A/B/C test: 3 variants, 5 primary metrics, full decision framework
Result
7-figure Annual revenue target if channel mix shifts as designed
10/10 Task completion rate in usability testing
P0 Shipped March 2026 — A/B/C test live
3 variants A/B/C test to measure channel mix impact at scale
