About this project
Situation

In December 2024, VistaPrint launched Order Help V1 to enable customers to manage orders without contacting support. Within weeks it was generating a significant increase in unnecessary contacts instead of reducing them — rolled back after one month. Root cause: 82% of users ended up waiting 24 hours for email responses, but the flow didn't reveal this until the final step. Users abandoned and used faster channels, creating follow-up cases.

Task

Diagnose what went wrong and lead the end-to-end redesign and expansion of the tool. Owned discovery, research, design, and testing across all phases. Led discovery independently when PM support was unavailable for the Q2 expansion flows.

Action
  • Analysed 200+ support cases and 50 follow-up cases to map root causes of V1 failure
  • Conducted usability testing with 35+ users across four distinct flows
  • Designed and shipped four self-service flows: relaunch (Q4), tracking (Q1), damaged and late/missing (Q2)
  • Led Q2 discovery independently — ran stakeholder interviews, analytics review, and research coordination without PM support
  • Pushed back on a decision not to create Salesforce cases for automated resolutions; escalated collectively with other designers; decision was reversed by leadership
Result
0.3% → 1.5%+ Deflection rate — all flows, still climbing
67% Tracking users resolve without a support ticket
4 flows Launched across 3 quarters
0% Follow-up case rate — fully eliminated
Delivers
  • Four end-to-end self-service flows: entry, tracking, resolve, escalate (Figma + dev-ready specs)
  • Usability test reports across all flows (35+ participants)
  • A/B test for design system component conflict — data-backed resolution
  • Design principles framework and stakeholder alignment documentation
Stakeholders managed
  • CARE Leadership — design principles sign-off and Salesforce case visibility rules
  • Engineering — feasibility constraints on Chat Anywhere dependency
  • Process & Ops — automation eligibility rules for reprint, refund, and store credit
  • Design System team — A/B test to resolve component conflict with data rather than opinion
Documentation prepared
  • PRD — co-authored with PM, including success metrics and acceptance criteria
  • Usability test plans and synthesis reports (all four flows)
  • A/B test specification for order status screen component decision
  • Design principles document for stakeholder alignment
