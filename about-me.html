The approach fits the problem — not a fixed template.

Different project types demand different phase emphasis. A 0→1 product needs deep discovery before a pixel is drawn. A redesign often has data already — the work is interpretation and synthesis. A design system is architecture first. Below is how I adapt.

Working style

Respectful pushback, with a solution

I challenge decisions when the evidence points the other way — but I come with an alternative, not just a concern. Stakeholders leave the conversation with options, not blockers.

Ship early, validate fast

Prefer a rough prototype in front of users over a polished design in a Figma file. Real feedback from real users beats internal debate every time.

Advocate for user AND business

Good design solves the user's problem in a way that moves a business metric. If the solution only does one, it's incomplete. I hold both in scope simultaneously.

Behavioural data over opinions

Analytics, support transcripts, and session recordings tell a more honest story than stakeholder intuition. I read the data first, then go to users to understand the why.

Accessible by default (WCAG 2.2)

Accessibility is a constraint I build with, not a checklist at the end. It makes the product better for everyone — not just the 15–20% of users who need it most.
