About this project
Situation

Roughly 50% of patients with chronic conditions don't take medication as prescribed (WHO). Existing tools — Medisafe, MyTherapy — treat this as a scheduling problem and get abandoned: 21% of survey respondents reported abandoning a reminder app. Primary research I ran (quantitative survey, n=127, distributed via patient community networks, plus 6 semi-structured interviews) found the real driver: 44% of respondents skip intentionally, not accidentally. Inductive coding of the interview transcripts surfaced five emotional root causes — fear of side effects, identity resistance ("taking it every day reminds me I am sick"), routine disruption, institutional mistrust, and a perceived-necessity gap ("when I feel fine, it doesn't feel like I need it"). No reminder app addresses any of them.

Task

Co-founded Tuza as a two-person MSc capstone team (Barcelona Technology School, Digital Product Management). I owned product and design end-to-end — discovery, primary research design and synthesis, problem framing, solution architecture, UX/UI design system, and the interactive prototype build. My co-founder, Ronald Ndatinya, owns community growth and patient acquisition — a separate track from the design work described here.

Action
  • Designed and ran a two-phase primary research study — quantitative survey (n=127) plus 6 semi-structured interviews — and inductively coded transcripts into 5 root-cause clusters
  • Synthesised findings into a Jobs to Be Done framework and a problem statement anchored on "Alex," a persona representing the 44% intentional-non-adherer segment
  • Architected the product as 4 sequential, hypothesis-driven pillars (AI Companion, Well-being PDF, Predictive Shield, Habit Studio) — each with an explicit if/then hypothesis, a validation threshold, and a pre-defined failure definition with a pivot path, rather than a single feature list
  • Designed and built a 25-route Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind prototype covering onboarding, daily check-in, the skip-reason flow, the AI companion chat, and 7-day/30-day progress views
  • Designed the skip-reason taxonomy as the core interaction primitive — each of the 5 emotional clusters routes to one of three AI response modes (Encouragement, Education, Empathy)
  • Wrote and iterated the AI companion's system prompt, including a 3-tier severity-triage safety layer (Emergency / Serious / Routine) and a doctor-attributed medication framing rule so the AI never substitutes its own clinical judgment for the prescriber's
  • Authored and enforced a design system (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md) — tokens, CTA and footer patterns, sticky-layout skeleton — applied consistently across all 25 routes
  • Modelled Year 1 unit economics and defined go/no-go validation gates (retention, NPS, export-rate thresholds) for each solution pillar
